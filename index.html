Process

Three points of contact from first call to launch. You always know which one we're in.

01

Let's talk

We go through what the site has to do, who it is for and what already exists. You leave with a page count, a timeline and a price.

02

Draw & refine

Wireframes first, then visual design. You see it at each stage and mark it up. Nothing gets built until the drawing is signed off.

03

Build & launch

We develop, test and move it onto your domain and hosting, then hand over the keys and stay on for maintenance.
