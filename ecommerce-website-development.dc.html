01

One project: storefront, checkout, catalogue

An online store is four things working together: the storefront people browse, the catalogue behind it, the cart, and a checkout that doesn’t lose the sale. We draw all four as wireframes and you approve them before the build starts — so scope, timeline and price are settled on paper first. How long it takes depends mostly on how ready your catalogue is; the plan puts a date on it.

02

WooCommerce, custom — or honestly, a builder

If you carry a modest catalogue and your team already works in WordPress, WooCommerce is usually the sensible answer — our WordPress services cover that side. If your store needs logic no plugin handles well, custom development earns its cost. And if you’re testing an idea with a handful of products, we’ll say so plainly: a simple store builder may be enough for now, and we’d rather you come back when you’ve outgrown it.

03

Payments that work in India

Indian customers pay by UPI first and cards second, and they abandon checkouts that make either hard. Payment gateway integration is part of every store we build: helping you choose a gateway that fits your bank and business type, wiring it into checkout, and testing the awkward cases — refunds, retries, dropped connections — before launch rather than after.

04

What you’ll need to prepare

The build moves only as fast as the catalogue. Before development you’ll need product names, prices, photographs and descriptions, plus two decisions many owners leave too late: how GST applies to your products, and who ships your orders, where to, at what rates. You get a checklist at the planning stage — and if descriptions are the bottleneck, our website content service writes them.

05

What it costs depends on

Two stores can differ in price by multiples for reasons that are easy to explain: how many products you carry, whether WooCommerce covers your needs or custom work is required, what your checkout has to integrate with, and how much of the design is drawn from scratch. We scope first and come back with a plan and a price — you’ll know what you’re paying for before you pay for it.

06

Built in Delhi NCR, handed over anywhere

We’re registered in Delhi NCR as WESTRA Solution, and store builds are delivered remotely to businesses across India. At launch you get the keys: admin access, a walkthrough, and notes your team can actually use. Running the store day to day — product uploads, order settings, ongoing changes — is its own service: e-commerce support. Hosting sized for stores lives under domain, hosting & business email.
