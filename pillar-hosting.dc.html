Hosting & Site Management

Nobody buys hosting because they want hosting. They want a site that loads, email that arrives, and someone to call when neither happens.

01

Cheap hosting is not cheap

The saving on a budget plan is smaller than one day of downtime during a campaign. What you are paying for is response time when something goes wrong.

02

Speed is a business number

Load time changes how many people stay, and it changes how you rank. If you are working on search visibility, performance is part of that work — see SEO Services.

03

Backups you have actually restored

An untested backup is a hope. We restore ours to check they work before you need them.
