What we design and build

11 areas — tap to expand
01 Website Design

How your website looks and how it works for a visitor: page layouts, colours, type, and how easily people find what they came for — the part designers call UI/UX. Every layout is drawn and shown to you before it becomes a real page.

02 Website Development

The build itself: turning approved designs into a fast, working website with clean code, forms that reach you, and pages that hold up on slow connections.

03 Business & Corporate Websites

Company sites that answer what buyers actually check: what you do, where you are, who you work with and how to reach you — organised so a first-time visitor understands it in one visit.

04 Responsive Website Design

One website that stays easy to use on phones, tablets and desktops. Menus, buttons and forms are sized for thumbs rather than shrunk from a desktop screen — most of your visitors will be on phones.

05 Custom Website Development

A site coded specifically for your needs when ready-made setups don’t fit — including lean static builds for sites that rarely change and need to load instantly.

06 CMS-Based Websites

Dynamic sites built on a content system with an admin panel, so your team adds pages, changes prices and publishes updates without calling a developer. The platform comparison further down this page shows when this is the right choice.

07 Website Redesign & Revamp

Refreshing an existing site’s design and content without losing what already works — your search rankings included. If the foundations are too old to be worth fixing, we tell you before you spend.

08 Landing Page Design

Single focused pages for a campaign, a launch or an ad: one message, one action, built to load fast and make the clicks you pay for count.

09 Web Portal Development

Websites people log into — student areas, dealer portals, member dashboards — with accounts, roles and content that changes depending on who is signed in.

10 Website Planning & Consultancy

The thinking before the building: which pages you actually need, what each one should say, and what the whole project should cost. Useful even if you end up building elsewhere.

11 Website Maintenance & Support

Keeping the site current after launch: content updates, small fixes, and a person who answers when something breaks.

All of this is available to businesses across Delhi NCR — Delhi, Noida, Gurugram, Ghaziabad, Faridabad — and, because the whole process runs remotely, to businesses anywhere in India.

How the work runs

5 stages
01

Discover

We start with your business, not the layout: what you sell, who buys, and what the website has to do for you. You leave this stage with a page list, a timeline and a price.

02

Design

Before any code, you get a simple drawing of every page showing what goes where — designers call it a wireframe. You mark it up, we adjust, and nothing is built until you approve it.

03

Develop

The approved drawings become a working website: custom-coded, or built on a content system you can edit yourself, whichever fits how you plan to run it.

04

Launch

Domain, hosting and business email get set up, and the site is checked on phones, tablets and desktops before it goes live.

05

Maintain

You get the keys and a proper walkthrough. Updates and fixes can stay with us or move in-house — your call, never a lock-in.

How should your website be built?

Three options, compared

CMS-based

Built on a content system with an admin panel, so your team edits pages and adds content without touching code.

Best for

Businesses that will publish, edit and grow the site themselves.

Who updates it

You do — from an admin panel, after a walkthrough from us.

Trade-off

Needs routine updates and security care. Most of our CMS builds run on WordPress.

Custom-coded

Written specifically for your site with nothing extra loaded, which keeps it lean and quick.

Best for

Sites that change rarely and where speed matters most.

Who updates it

We do — changes go through a developer.

Trade-off

Every edit is a code edit. Fine twice a year; costly twice a week.

DIY builders

The drag-and-drop tools you have seen advertised. Honest answer: sometimes they are enough.

Best for

Testing an idea with a few pages before committing a budget.

Who updates it

You do, inside the builder’s own editor.

Trade-off

Quick to start, limiting to grow. If this is all you need right now, we will say so.

Decisions worth making early

Before you brief anyone
01

Redesign or rebuild

An existing site isn’t always a teardown. If the structure is sound, a redesign refreshes layout and content on what you have. If the foundations fight every change, rebuilding costs less than renovating. We audit first and show you which side your site falls on.

02

Built for phones first

Most of your visitors will arrive on a phone, so we design the phone view as a first-class layout, not a squeezed copy of the desktop. Menus, forms and buttons are drawn for thumbs, and every page plan you approve shows both views.

03

What you bring, what we handle

You bring the business knowledge: what you sell, who buys, what makes you different. We handle structure, design and build. Words for the pages can come from you — or from our website content service if writing isn’t your job.

04

What affects the price

Two websites can differ in cost for reasons you can check yourself: how many pages carry real content, whether the design is drawn fresh or adapted, whether you need help with writing, and what the site has to connect to. We scope first, then quote one fixed price you approve before the build starts.

Questions owners ask us

Before you call
How much does a website cost in India?

There is no honest single number — the market runs from a few thousand rupees for a template to several lakhs for large custom platforms. What moves your price: how many pages carry real content, custom design versus adapted, writing help, and integrations. We give you the full figure after a short scoping call, before anything is built.

How long does a website take?

The plan you approve carries the dates, so you are never guessing. In practice the schedule depends less on the build and more on how ready your content is — text and photos are where most projects lose time. If a launch date matters, say so in the first call and we plan backwards from it.

Will I be able to update the website myself?

On a CMS build, yes: you get an admin login and a walkthrough, and everyday edits need no developer. Custom-coded sites trade that for speed — changes go through us. The comparison above shows which fits how you work.

Do you only work with businesses in Delhi NCR?

No. We are registered in Delhi NCR as WESTRA Solution, and the whole process runs remotely, first call to launch, so businesses anywhere in India get the same service.
